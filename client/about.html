What is Campus Event Tracker?

Campus Event Tracker is a comprehensive web-based platform designed to streamline the process of discovering, hosting, and participating in college events. In many educational institutions, event information is scattered across WhatsApp groups, notice boards, and word-of-mouth — making it easy for students to miss opportunities. Our platform centralizes all event information in one beautiful, easy-to-use interface.

Whether you're an organizer looking to reach more students, or a participant searching for events that match your interests, Campus Event Tracker has you covered. With features like smart search, category filtering, real-time join counters, and a completely offline-capable architecture, this platform is built for the real needs of campus communities.
